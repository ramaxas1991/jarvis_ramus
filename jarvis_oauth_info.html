About this application

Built by one person, for one person

Jarvis is a personal software project developed and operated solely by Ramunas. It was created to automate small personal tasks — such as managing calendars, reminders, and private notes — and is not distributed, marketed, or made available to the general public.

There is no sign-up process, no public user base, and no commercial activity associated with this application. Jarvis authenticates exclusively with its creator's own Google account in order to access the specific personal services it was built to assist with.

i Jarvis is a private-use tool. It is not intended for, and is not accessible by, members of the public.
